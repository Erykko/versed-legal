## Subscriptions

### What does Versed Plus include?

Unlimited verse explanations (plain-language meaning, historical context and original Hebrew and Greek words) and every reading plan. Reading the Bible in Versed is always free, with no limits.

### How does the free trial work?

New subscribers to the yearly plan can try Versed Plus free for 7 days. Two days before the trial ends we send a notification reminding you, if you allowed notifications. If you don't cancel, the yearly subscription starts when the trial ends. Apple and Google decide who is eligible for a trial.

### How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, so you cancel in your store account, not in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge.

- **iPhone or iPad:** open **Settings**, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, choose **Versed** and tap **Cancel Subscription**.
- **Android:** open the **Google Play Store**, tap your profile picture, then **Payments & subscriptions** → **Subscriptions**, choose **Versed** and tap **Cancel subscription**.

You keep Versed Plus until the end of the period you've already paid for.

### Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google process all payments, so refunds go through them:

- **Apple:** request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **Google Play:** see [Google Play refunds](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

### I paid but Plus isn't unlocked

Open the paywall or your Account page and tap **Restore purchases**. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with. If it still doesn't work, email us with the date of purchase.

## Explanations

### How many free explanations do I get?

Three new explanations each week, refreshed every Monday. Reopening one you've already read is free. Versed Plus removes the limit.

### Are the explanations always right?

Explanations are written by AI (Claude, by Anthropic) and aim to reflect mainstream Christian understanding. They can still be wrong or incomplete, so read them alongside the full chapter and your church's teaching. If you spot a mistake, email us the verse and what looks wrong.

## Reading

### Which translations are available?

The World English Bible (WEB), King James Version (KJV), Bible in Basic English (BBE) and American Standard Version (ASV). All are in the public domain. Switch translations from the book picker at the top of the feed.

### Where is my reading progress saved?

Your place in the Bible, saved verses and streak are stored on your device. They don't move to a new phone yet.

## Your account

### Do I need an account?

No. You can read without one. Signing in with Google ties your subscription to your account so it can follow you.

### How do I delete my account?

In the app, open **Account** (the person icon at the top of the feed) and tap **Delete account**. See [Delete your account](../delete-account/) for other options and what gets deleted.
